The problem

The barrier that protects the brain
also keeps medicine out.

Most CNS gene therapies are delivered by a surgical route — intrathecal, intracisternal, intraparenchymal — and then spread by diffusion. Coverage is strongest at the injection site and uneven everywhere else. We took the route the body already maintains.

01 / BIOLOGY

A transporter, not a hole

TfR1 ferries iron-loaded transferrin across the blood-brain barrier by receptor-mediated transcytosis, continuously, throughout life. TfR1 CapX binds the apical domain — away from the transferrin site — and rides along.

02 / VASCULATURE

No cell is far from a vessel

No cell in the mammalian brain sits more than about 20 µm from a blood vessel. Reaching the vasculature means reaching the whole CNS, and because all mammalian brains are similarly vascularized, IV dosing scales between species.

03 / TRANSLATION

Engineered on human biology

The capsid was selected against the human receptor from the start, rather than discovered in non-human primates and back-mapped to people. TfR1 itself carries 40+ years of research, an approved therapy, and active clinical programs.

Mechanism

Receptor-targeted transcytosis, step by step

  1. BindThe capsid attaches to TfR1 on the blood-facing surface of a brain endothelial cell.
  2. InternalizeReceptor and cargo are drawn into an endosome.
  3. ReleaseThe endosome crosses the cell and releases the capsid on the brain side.
  4. RecycleThe receptor returns to the surface and the cycle repeats — iron uptake is never blocked.

Access

Open Aperture: the capsid, at no cost, for academic labs

Qualified academic researchers can get TfR1 CapX for non-commercial work through a streamlined material transfer agreement. The aim is to cut the time between a promising idea and a rigorous experiment.

Along with the capsid, participating labs receive a preclinical development roadmap, a CMC documentation package, and introductions to CDMO and CRO vendors already working with TfR1 CapX.

Program details & application

Who qualifies

Principal investigators and research teams at accredited academic institutions, pursuing non-commercial research. Not affiliated with one? Write to us about other access routes.

What it costs

Nothing. Eligible labs receive the capsid at no cost; up to five indications per application, and more can be added later.
