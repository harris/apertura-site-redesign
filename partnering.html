Ways to work together

Four routes to the capsid

Capsid licensing

License TfR1 CapX for a defined indication and take your own payload into the clinic. AviadoBio, Finding Hope for Frizzle and Lexeo Therapeutics have done exactly this.

Foundation & advocacy collaborations

Disease foundations bring the target, the patients and the urgency. The TSC Alliance, the Rett Syndrome Research Trust and FAST work with us this way.

Academic & government research

Cooperative agreements with institutions such as the NIH and the Broad Institute, plus no-cost academic access through Open Aperture.

Manufacturing & CMC

A strategic collaboration with Viralgen supports scalable TfR1 CapX manufacturing, and partners inherit the analytics and CMC groundwork already laid.
